## Q1 — Problem

Mobile RAG alternates between long-context prefill and one-token decode. Static NPU graphs make phase switching expensive; memory-bound decode underutilizes matrix hardware. The paper reports graph switching can account for up to 29% of decode latency in its tested RAG workloads.

## Q2 — Mechanisms

1. **Progressive graph scheduling:** partition the model into blocks and overlap decoding-graph loading with chunked prefill, amortizing the transition rather than performing a blocking full graph swap.
2. **In-context distribution calibration:** reuse logits already produced during prefill to calibrate retrieved draft candidates to the model's distribution.
3. **Verification-aware draft extension:** recycle high-confidence rejected draft tokens from earlier steps to lengthen later verification batches and improve NPU utilization.

These are runtime, graph and speculative-decoding techniques; no new CPU structure is proposed.

## Q3 — Experimental scope and results

- Three Qualcomm phones: Redmi K60 Pro (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2), Redmi K70 Pro (Snapdragon 8 Gen 3), OnePlus 13 (Snapdragon 8 Elite).
- Models: Qwen2.5-1.5B, Qwen2.5-0.5B, Llama 3.2-3B.
- Tasks: document summarization, RAG QA, UI automation and automatic message reply.
- Implemented on mllm.
- Reports 1.06–3.81× end-to-end speedup and 1.11–4.18× energy reduction versus the vanilla system; up to 2.53× latency and 4.71× energy improvement versus existing speculative-decoding integrations.

Keep these comparisons separate: the broad 1.06–3.81× and 1.11–4.18× figures are not the same denominator as the maximum improvements against other speculative-decoding integrations.

## Q4 — Relevance and limitations

This is an important older baseline that the previous repository inventory did not contain. It already covers commercial-phone RAG and UI automation, NPU graph switching, speculative decoding and energy. It therefore narrows any claim that NPU-only RAG/Agent acceleration or NPU-tailored speculative execution is a brand-new opportunity in 2026.

However, it is not a head-to-head comparison with Tandem: models, workloads, phones, runtime and metrics differ. The shared Peking University affiliation with HeRo/Agent.xpu is an institutional overlap only; author/lab lineage and code independence require a separate check before claiming independent replication.

## Q5 — Decision impact

**Strengthen C/CG-06 as a software/runtime track; narrow B-residual only for graph-switching and inference-state reuse.** It does not prove that cross-CPU/NPU/DRAM physical state consistency is solved, nor that a new hardware primitive is required.

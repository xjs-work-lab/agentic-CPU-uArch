## Anti-drift and method
No experiment required for research completion. Published source results bounded by original test conditions and vendor claims distinct from independent results. Different hardware OEMs do not equal independent CPU chip mechanisms. `score_context` old A/R1 historical numbers are not probabilities. High product value can motivate engineering investment even where independent research novelty is crowded. Hardware-specific positive claim is not justified by software-level success.

# Active 2027–2029 Mobile Agentic CPU/SoC Strategic Roadmap — Round15E

Current: 2026-10-08. Public-evidence-only, **zero owned experiments/test/PoC/simulation**.
Authoritative portfolio [current.md](current.md); decision [DEC-PORTFOLIO-002](../08-decisions/events/DEC-PORTFOLIO-002.md); [three-theme cross-AO model](../analysis/opportunities/round15b-cross-opportunity-synthesis-2026-10-08.md).

## Leadership decision in 30 seconds
**BUILD/INVEST three engineering/platform capacities, keep three differentiated research reserves, and approve no new Agent-only CPU-uArch or silicon bet today.** This is a deliberate foresight result based on technical/public evidence, not avoidance of an experimental gate.

### Structural mobile Agentic workload shifts (2027–2029 strategic ranking)
1. **Persistent Agent task/session/tool workflow, cross-app action and trust** (high public product+academic support; mainstream persistence extent future conditional).
2. **Repeated short heterogeneous CPU control ↔ AI stage/operator handoffs** (published Snapdragon stage crossover, Agentic workflow system and OS/NPU scheduling; backend evolution can change winner).
3. **Revised/interruptible/transactional actions and versioned derived state** (academic software strong, direct target-phone frequency/cost lower confidence).
4. **Proactive background sensing and meaningful 'do not act' suppression** (vendor dual LP NPU/CHRE, emerging product uptake; matched daily energy versus helpfulness still low direct confidence).

### Three cross-layer architecture **themes** (not three silicon bets)
- **F Execution–State Lifecycle:** priority, preemption and model admission (AOSP), source/permission/effect and derived memory validity (Agent software), buffer/queue lifetime (QNN/HAL). Strong baseline; reserve narrowed only to cross-model/engine version-aware physical residual **B/R2**.
- **P Proactive Admission:** event→no-action→intent→active escalation under trust, battery and foreground QoE. CHRE/AOP/MediaTek/Qualcomm already relevant; incremental Agent-only LP hardware not established. **CG-07 follow**.
- **V Semantic Progress / QoE Policy:** runtime/OS inference from full ledger, user history, utility, permission, verification + optimized model progress (LAS/ProgRouter); **A private RequiredProgress only reserve**, no new ISA.

- **Differentiated Primary Bet supported from public evidence: 0**. A historical 82.5 is retired as an allocation score and moved to **conditional reserve**.

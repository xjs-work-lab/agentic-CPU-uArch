## Why
The strongest-baseline chain captures every broad H-PAM mechanism examined:
- MUSE: smartphone heterogeneous retrieval + insertion/index maintenance;
- LEANN / CD-ANN: compact/dynamic index software;
- MobiMem / AgeMem: explicit memory semantics consumed by Agent/runtime software;
- SwiftMem: temporal/semantic routing + co-consolidation/layout;

- MMEdge: sensing/encoding pipeline coupling and modality-aware runtime adaptation;

No direct evidence establishes a distinct lower-level Agent-memory control variable surviving these routes.

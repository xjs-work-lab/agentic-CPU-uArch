# DEC-CG07-002 — Proactive workload strengthens CG-07 relevance but raises its baseline

## Change
CG-07 remains **EXPLORE / 75.0 / architecture benchmark + co-design study**.

The workload premise is strengthened, but the architecture residual is narrowed.

## Why
- PAPER-087 establishes proactive mobile assistance as an explicit intervene/no-intervene workload over ongoing context;
- PAPER-088 shows that a lightweight pre-reasoning front end can suppress a large fraction of heavy reasoning in the evaluated benchmark;
- therefore “always-on Agent” is no longer sufficient hardware justification;
- CG-07 must beat the residual after software/model sparsification.

## Experiment consequence
EXP-CG07-001 now models:
`context observation → gate → accepted subset → heavy reasoner`

and requires measured gate, acceptance, handoff/wake and idle/residency inputs.

## Boundary
No SYSTEM_VALUE. No score promotion. No dedicated-domain hardware commitment. No second differentiated Primary Bet created.

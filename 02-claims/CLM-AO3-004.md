# CLM-AO3-004

## Proposition
Leading mobile compute vendors publicly increase or flexibilize on-chip/shared state capacity and system-level coherence while explicitly associating those mechanisms with Agentic, long-context or multi-step workloads, indicating that working-set residency and handoff are becoming product architecture concerns.

## Boundary
Vendor disclosures establish product direction and capability, not independent proof of Agent-specific value or a unified Agent state fabric.

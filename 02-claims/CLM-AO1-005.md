# CLM-AO1-005

## Proposition
Broad heterogeneous task placement, cache-demand-aware migration and planner/workflow-to-resource scheduling are already crowded generic prior-art families; AO-1 cannot claim differentiation merely from Agent-aware scheduling or moving work to the right processor.

## Boundary
The audited patents constrain broad mechanism novelty. They do not close a narrower future opportunity in low-overhead Agent execution/state handoff across a mobile heterogeneous fabric.

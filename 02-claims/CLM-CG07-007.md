# CLM-CG07-007

## Proposition
PRPF reports that lightweight pre-reasoning intervention gating plus candidate-function compression can substantially reduce heavy proactive-Agent execution before specialized always-on hardware is considered.

## Current interpretation
On ProactiveMobile, PAPER-088 reports Success Rate 20.82% → 41.15%, False Trigger Rate 13.76% → 7.21%, expected inference compute −69.3% and end-to-end latency −60.1% versus its ProactiveMobile 7B baseline.

This materially raises the CG-07 strongest baseline.

## Boundary
PAPER-088 is a 2026 preprint and its efficiency results are GPU/benchmark results, not commercial-smartphone battery, rail-power, wake/residency or thermal SYSTEM_VALUE.

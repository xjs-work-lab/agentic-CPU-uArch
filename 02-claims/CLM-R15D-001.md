# CLM-R15D-001

## Proposition
Direct independent/dependent claim review establishes old patent coverage for three broadly framed control families: cache-demand-weighted mobile CPU-cluster migration (US9626295B2), version/identity-valid dialogue/reasoning cache with current-demand satisfaction (CN121960775A), and coordinator-managed multi-Agent prepare/commit/rollback/state migration (CN120704926A).

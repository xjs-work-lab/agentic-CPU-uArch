# CLM-MOBILE-006

## Proposition
High-level application/context requirements driving dynamic low-level sensing activation, shared/incremental processing and processing-vs-energy policies are established mobile-systems prior art and cannot by themselves establish Agent-memory differentiation.

## Evidence
PAPER-078 / SeeMon, MobiSys 2008.

## Boundary
Historical hardware and physical-sensor modalities differ from modern Agent screenshot/A11y/event capture.
The prior-art pressure applies to the **control abstraction**, not to modern performance magnitude.

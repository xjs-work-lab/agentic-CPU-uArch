# CLM-XAO-001

## Proposition
AO-1 execution/dispatch handoff, AO-2 revision/effect authority, and AO-3 derived-state version/residency are mutually interacting lifecycle boundaries around one heterogenous Agent execution, but they are analytically distinct control and correctness responsibilities rather than three independently justified CPU-uArch investments.

## Boundary
Shared architecture-search boundary is an analyst synthesis. No phone source establishes a unified fabric as necessary; do not collapse legal authority into cache residency or count one vendor cache source as three independent hardware confirmations.

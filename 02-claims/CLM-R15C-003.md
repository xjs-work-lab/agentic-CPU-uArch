# CLM-R15C-003 — SUPPORTED

## Proposition
Android has deprecated NNAPI NDK in Android 15 while retaining vendor HAL interfaces and moving application ML integration toward updatable runtime/AI services; AICore Gemini Nano provides on-device inference, model management and restricted package/privacy handling. The foremost near-term opportunity is portable compiler/runtime/OS contracts rather than a frozen legacy NNAPI app API.

## Boundary
AICore system capability and runtime migration are official platform guidance, not evidence of arbitrary third-party private Agent-state access, complete privacy protection or differentiated new silicon.

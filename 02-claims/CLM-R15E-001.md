# CLM-R15E-001 — Public-source decision gate for a differentiated Primary Bet

**Proposition:** Strong public evidence for the *usefulness of software-derived Agent progress and resource control* does not establish the specific non-reconstructible private `RequiredProgress` incremental information required by A's differentiated thesis, and does not establish a hardware-uArch value. Under a public-only investment standard, A is therefore **not supportable as an active differentiated Primary Bet** but remains a conditional research Reserve. This is a **portfolio sufficiency judgement**, not an empirical negative showing private-state value equals zero.

**Decision boundary:** Prior original papers used software-observed coordinator/history/validator features, differing server/GPU/phone environments and incomparable utilities; none conducts a matched-visibility private signal isolation and none proves novel Agent-specific silicon. The absence of that demonstration cannot be used to kill continued research or declare no eventual value.

# CLM-CAL-002

## Proposition
Sustained multi-billion-parameter LLM fine-tuning is feasible on a modern smartphone but can impose material battery, thermal-throttling and backward-pass runtime cost over complete training runs.

## Current interpretation
PAPER-091 supplies direct phone SYSTEM_VALUE evidence for training as a sustained workload rather than a single-step microbenchmark.

## Boundary
This is generic on-device training evidence; it does not by itself establish an Agent-specific control variable.

# CLM-MOBILE-007

## Proposition
Fine-grained sensing-to-encoding pipelining, modality-aware runtime configuration and cross-modal work skipping based on timing/resource/confidence signals are established on-device systems mechanisms and cannot by themselves establish persistent Agent-memory differentiation.

## Evidence
PAPER-079 / MMEdge, SenSys 2026.

## Boundary
MMEdge is not a personal-memory workload.
The Claim constrains **mechanism novelty**, not target-workload magnitude or future Agent-specific residuals.

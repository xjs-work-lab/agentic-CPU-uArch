# CLM-T6-003

## Proposition
Android 16+ AppFunctions provides an OS/Jetpack substrate in which app-defined functions can be indexed, discovered and executed locally by authorized Agent callers with explicit metadata, enabled state and access/permission control; AndroidX 1.0.0-alpha12 further adds experimental runtime function-registration APIs.

## Current interpretation
This is direct official evidence that local Agent-callable app functions are becoming a first-class Android platform capability.

## Boundary
AppFunctions remains experimental/limited-access in the current product cycle and does not by itself establish large-scale retail-phone workload frequency or cost.

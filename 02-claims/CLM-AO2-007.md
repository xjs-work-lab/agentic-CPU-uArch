# CLM-AO2-007

## Hypothesis
If lower-layer revision/speculation cost becomes material, plausible co-design candidates include lightweight execution-version or epoch identifiers, cancellable accelerator work, rapid state/resource reclamation, selective state retirement/inheritance, and low-overhead shadow/checkpoint support.

## Boundary
These are Architecture Hypotheses only. Existing papers do not establish any of them as necessary silicon features.

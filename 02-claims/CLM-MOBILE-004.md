# CLM-MOBILE-004

## Proposition
On evaluated commercial smartphone SoCs, high-dimensional semantic retrieval coexisting with continuous ingestion and index maintenance creates material data-movement, layout-conversion and heterogeneous CPU/GPU/NPU execution costs; hardware-aware index/layout/pipeline/scheduling choices materially change throughput, energy and thermal behavior.

## Evidence
PAPER-069 / MUSE (ACM Multimedia 2026).

## Boundary
MUSE is a generic multimedia/mobile retrieval system.

This Claim does not establish:
- that the workload is uniquely Agentic;
- that M3-Agent-like memory has the same operation mix;
- Huawei transfer;
- a CPU/uArch-specific mechanism.

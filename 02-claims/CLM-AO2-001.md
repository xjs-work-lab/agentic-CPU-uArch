# CLM-AO2-001

## Proposition
Agent systems can create useful work before final authority is resolved: actions may be speculative, revised, cancelled or discarded, while externally visible effects require a later commit/authorization boundary.

## Boundary
Direct evidence is runtime/environment-level. It does not establish mobile accelerator or CPU hardware value.

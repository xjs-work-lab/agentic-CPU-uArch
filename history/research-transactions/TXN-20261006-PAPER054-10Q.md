## Core interpretation
TimelyLLM exploits **pre-ready future-use timing**:
generate the first actionable segment, then schedule later generation around physical execution slack.

This is adjacent to but not identical with R1, whose surviving residual begins **after the dependency is already ready**.

## Decision
- PAPER-054: KEEP / P0.
- R1: no lane/score change; broad semantic timing novelty is further narrowed and B4-release baseline strengthened.
- A: no lane/score change; no direct DemandState evidence.
- C: no lane/score change; problem relevance and upper-layer software capture both strengthened.
- R3: remains BLOCKED.
- Broad "Execution-Time Utility Control" is **not promoted** to a new Direction/Primary Bet.
- Second differentiated Primary Bet remains unfilled.

## Insight
Time-indexed utility of Agent outputs is a real control signal, but TimelyLLM demonstrates that much of this signal can already be consumed at the serving-runtime layer. A differentiated smartphone/system/hardware direction must therefore prove a residual beyond segmented/slack-aware software scheduling.
